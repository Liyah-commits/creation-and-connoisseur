We are planning a mobile food pantry, targeted to begin monthly distributions in 2027. Until then, here is how to get food this week and how to reach us.

## Food you can get now

- Dial 211 or visit [Nevada 211](https://www.nevada211.org/food-assistance/) for food pantries, meal programs and emergency help near you, in English or Spanish.
- [Three Square Food Bank](https://www.threesquare.org/find-food) runs pantries and distribution sites across Southern Nevada. Use their site to find the closest one.
- UNLV students can use the campus food pantry. Check [UNLV's website](https://www.unlv.edu/unit/food-pantry) for current hours and location.
- SNAP (food stamps) applications are at [Access Nevada](https://accessnevada.nv.gov/). Anyone can apply; we are happy to help you with the form.

## How to reach us

Use the form below or the email on our [Contact page](/contact/). Tell us what you need, how many people are in your household, and the best way to reach you. We reply within two business days. There is no paperwork, no proof of income, and no judgment. Everything you tell us stays private.

## When the pantry is running

The pantry is planned for 2027. The plan is that anyone can come to any distribution, and no one will be asked for ID or documents. Bring bags if you have them; we plan to have some for people who do not. We plan for staff and volunteers at distributions to speak English and Spanish.
