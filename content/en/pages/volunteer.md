## Volunteer roles

### Service volunteer

Join a volunteer day sorting, packing or serving food with a partner organization. No experience needed.

**Commitment:** 2 to 3 hours, as often as you like.

### Culture Night host

Represent your culture at an event: plan the menu, lead the cooking, and tell the story behind the food.

**Commitment:** one event, about 10 hours including planning.

### Event crew

Set up, serve, and clean up at Culture Nights. The fastest way to get involved.

**Commitment:** 3 to 4 hours per event.

### Pantry team (planned for 2027)

Once our planned mobile food pantry launches (targeted for 2027), help run its monthly distributions: loading, setup, greeting neighbors, and handing out food. Spanish speakers especially welcome.

**Commitment:** 4 hours, once a month, once the pantry is running.

### Skills volunteer

Lend a professional skill: photography, design, bookkeeping, grant writing, translation, or driving.

**Commitment:** flexible, project based.
