## Our goal

Our goal is for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. Where it costs nothing extra, we also follow the newer WCAG 2.2 requirements on visible focus, target size and dragging.

## What we do

- Every page is available in English and Spanish.
- You can use every part of the site with a keyboard alone.
- Text and controls meet the WCAG color contrast ratios.
- You can zoom pages to 200 percent without scrolling sideways.
- The site respects your device's "reduce motion" setting.
- We do not use an accessibility overlay or plug-in.

## How we test

Each time the site changes, every page is checked in both languages with automated tools: axe, Pa11y and Lighthouse. Testing with a keyboard, screen readers and zoom is part of our launch checklist.

## Known limitations

The content editing area at /keystatic, which only the site's editors use, is made by a third party and is not covered by this statement.

## Tell us about a problem

If something on this site doesn't work for you, please tell us on our [contact page](/contact/). Let us know the page address and what happened.
