On October 22, 2026, the UNLV Food Tasting Club hosted Taste of the Philippines, a culture night celebrating Filipino food and culture. Dinner came from Tina's Filipino Cuisine, and volunteers served plates of pancit noodles and grilled meat.

A student speaker shared her experience as a Filipina, and the evening also included a round of classic childhood games, including limbo.
