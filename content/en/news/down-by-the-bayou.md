On October 23, 2025, the UNLV Food Tasting Club hosted Down By The Bayou, a New Orleans themed dine-in movie night featuring *The Princess and the Frog*.

The night reached maximum capacity. Guests had powdered sugar beignets, and a large group posed together in front of the screen.
