On November 6, 2026, volunteers from the UNLV Food Tasting Club spent the afternoon at Three Square Food Bank, working a shift from 1:30 to 3:30 p.m.

In those two hours, the group helped feed more than 100 families. Three Square is one of the partners our volunteers serve with. To join a future volunteer day, see [Volunteer Days](/programs/volunteer-days/) or [sign up to volunteer](/volunteer/).
