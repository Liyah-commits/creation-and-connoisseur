We are planning a mobile food pantry, targeted for 2027, that would bring free groceries directly to UNLV students and to Las Vegas neighborhoods where getting to a food bank is hard. The plan is for monthly distributions with no paperwork and no proof of income, open to anyone who needs food.

**What we plan to stock:** shelf stable staples and fresh produce, chosen to match what the households at each site actually cook. Food that gets eaten is the only kind that counts, so we will ask, listen, and adjust the list.

**Who it is for:** any household that needs food.
