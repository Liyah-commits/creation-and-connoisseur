The UNLV Food Tasting Club is our campus program and the front door to everything we do. Student members gather every month for culture nights and campus food events that celebrate the cuisines of the communities that make up Las Vegas. Those events are also where our volunteers sign up and where we raise money toward the mobile food pantry we are planning for 2027. Join the club, and you are part of the mission.

**Who it is for:** UNLV students; events are open to the public.
