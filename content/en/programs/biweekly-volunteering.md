Every other week our volunteers serve alongside partner organizations that feed people in Las Vegas: sorting and packing food, serving meals, and helping at distributions. No experience is needed. Shifts are usually two to three hours, and we coordinate carpools from campus.

**Who it is for:** anyone 16 or older. Volunteers under 18 need a signed parent or guardian consent form.
